Before you come in

A few things
you might be wondering.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any relevant dental records, a list of medicines, and your questions. Contact the clinic first if you are unsure what is needed.

Can I ask about costs before treatment?

Yes. Ask for the proposed treatment plan and an estimate before deciding how to proceed.

I feel nervous about dental visits. Can I mention it?

Absolutely. Let the clinic know when arranging your visit so the team can discuss how to support you.
