Check-ups & cleaning

A routine visit gives us time to listen to your concerns and examine your teeth and gums. Your dentist will discuss whether cleaning, imaging, or follow-up care is appropriate.

02

Tooth repair

After examining the tooth, your dentist can explain suitable treatment options, their limitations, and what the next steps may involve.

03

Smile consultations

Tell us what you would like to change about your smile. We will discuss your goals and assess which options may be suitable before recommending treatment.

A few things
you might be wondering.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any relevant dental records, a list of medicines, and your questions. Contact the clinic first if you are unsure what is needed.

Can I ask about costs before treatment?

Yes. Ask for the proposed treatment plan and an estimate before deciding how to proceed.

I feel nervous about dental visits. Can I mention it?

Absolutely. Let the clinic know when arranging your visit so the team can discuss how to support you.
